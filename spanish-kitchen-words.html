30 kitchen words in Spanish, with Mexico and Spain versions

The kitchen is one of the best places to start learning Spanish: everything in it gets used every day, so the words come up again and again.

Here are 30 of the most useful kitchen words. Spanish changes a lot from country to country, so where Mexico and Spain use different words, you'll see both.

Learn these on your own things. Point Lablo at your kitchen and every word gets labeled, with audio.

Use them in a sentence

  • La leche está en el refrigerador.
    The milk is in the fridge.
  • Pon el sartén en la estufa.
    Put the frying pan on the stove.
  • ¿Me pasas un vaso de agua?
    Can you pass me a glass of water?
  • Los platos sucios están en el fregadero.
    The dirty dishes are in the sink.
  • Corta el pan en la tabla de cortar.
    Cut the bread on the cutting board.

In Spain, "la cocina" means both the kitchen and the stove. In Mexico, the stove is "la estufa," so "la cocina" is just the room.

Questions

How do you say kitchen in Spanish?

The kitchen is "la cocina." In Spain the same word is also used for the stove.

Is it el sartén or la sartén?

Both are heard. Spain and standard Spanish use "la sartén," while many people in Mexico and Central America say "el sartén."

What is the fastest way to remember kitchen words?

Learn them on the real objects. Scanning your own kitchen with Lablo labels each thing with its Spanish word, so you see the word every time you reach for it.
