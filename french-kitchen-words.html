French kitchen words are a great first set: you'll use them every day, and they help you get used to le and la.

Each word below includes its article, so you learn whether it's masculine or feminine along with the word itself.

Use them in a sentence

  • Le lait est dans le frigo.
    The milk is in the fridge.
  • Mets la poêle sur la cuisinière.
    Put the frying pan on the stove.
  • Tu peux me passer un verre ?
    Can you pass me a glass?
  • Les assiettes sont dans le placard.
    The plates are in the cupboard.

"Une casserole" is a saucepan, not a casserole dish. A frying pan is "une poêle."

Questions

How do you say kitchen in French?

The kitchen is "la cuisine." The same word also means cooking or cuisine.

Is fridge masculine or feminine in French?

Masculine: "le réfrigérateur," usually shortened to "le frigo."

How do I remember if a word is le or la?

Always learn the article with the word. Lablo shows every word with its article and reads it aloud, so le and la stick naturally.
