How it works

📷

Point & scan

Aim your camera at objects around you and tap. Lablo labels each one in the language you're learning.

🗣️

Hear every word

Translations, natural example sentences, and audio pronunciation for everything you catch.

📖

Build collections

Every word fills your collections — Kitchen, Food & Drink, Around Town, and more. It's a scavenger hunt for vocabulary.

🔥

A nudge every day

A fresh daily challenge keeps things fun and keeps your streak alive. Learn a little, every day.

🌍

Made for travelers & the curious

12 languages with regional variety. Scan menus and signs abroad, or just learn the words in your own home.
