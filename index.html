What makes Lablo different

Most camera apps tell you a cup is "la taza." Lablo tells you about your cup.

a scavenger hunt for words

Every room is a collection to fill

Words you catch drop into collections like Kitchen, Living Room and Around Town, plus odd ones like Round Things and Things That Make Noise. Finish one and you earn its badge.

And every word comes with a sentence written from your own photo, like the TV on the white wall or the plant by the window. That's what makes them stick.

Questions

Is Lablo free?

Yes. Lablo is free to download and includes 15 scans every day. Your words, collections and progress are always yours.

Which languages can I learn?

Spanish (Mexico or Spain), French, Italian, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin Chinese (Simplified or Traditional), Arabic, Hindi, Russian and Dutch.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, pick your language and start scanning. There's nothing to sign up for.

How is this different from a translator app?

A translator gives you an answer and you move on. Lablo helps you keep the words: it labels the objects in your own photo, writes example sentences about that exact scene, saves words to collections, and quizzes you on them later.

Does it work offline?

Scanning needs an internet connection. Your saved words, collections and progress are stored on your phone.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are only used to create your labels and sentences. They aren't sold, used for ads, or linked to your identity. The privacy policy has the details.
