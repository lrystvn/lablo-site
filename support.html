Frequently asked questions

How does Lablo work?

Point your camera at objects and tap the scan button. Lablo identifies each object and shows you what it's called in the language you're learning, with a translation, an example sentence, and audio pronunciation.

Do I need an account?

No. Just open the app and start learning. Your words, collections, and progress are saved right on your device.

How many scans do I get?

You get 15 free scans every day, which reset each day. Your saved words and collections are always yours.

Why isn't my scan working?

Lablo needs camera access and an internet connection to analyze your photos. If a scan doesn't work, check that camera access is enabled in your phone's Settings → Lablo → Camera, make sure you're connected to the internet, and try closing and reopening the app. You can also use the Upload button to scan an existing photo.

What languages does Lablo support?

Lablo supports 12 languages with regional variety, including Spanish, French, Italian, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Mandarin, Korean, and more. Switch anytime in the app.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your learning data stays on your device, and we don't sell your data or use it for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Will there be a premium version?

Lablo Unlimited is coming soon — with unlimited scans and more. For now, enjoy everything Lablo offers, free.
