Living room words in Spanish

Your living room is a great place to practice: you sit in it every day, and every object has a name worth knowing.

In most of Latin America the living room is "la sala." In Spain it's usually "el salón." Here are 26 words for the things inside it.

Learn these on your own things. Point Lablo at your living room and every word gets labeled, with audio.

Use them in a sentence

  • La televisión está en la pared.
    The TV is on the wall.
  • ¿Dónde está el control remoto?
    Where is the remote?
  • El gato duerme en el sofá.
    The cat sleeps on the couch.
  • Hay una planta junto a la ventana.
    There's a plant next to the window.

Careful with "el librero": it's a bookcase in Mexico, but in Spain it usually means a bookseller.

Questions

How do you say living room in Spanish?

"La sala" in Mexico and most of Latin America, "el salón" in Spain.

How do you say remote control in Spanish?

"El control remoto" in Mexico and much of Latin America. In Spain people say "el mando" or "el mando a distancia."

How can I practice these words at home?

Point Lablo at your living room. It labels the couch, the lamp, the plant and everything else in Spanish, with audio and a sentence about your room.
